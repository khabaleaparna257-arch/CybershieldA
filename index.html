CYBER AWARENESS

Know the Common Fraud Types

🎣

Phishing

Fake links or messages designed to trick users.

💸

Payment Fraud

Scams involving payments or fake requests.

🛒

Shopping Scam

Fake stores, offers or products.

💼

Job Scam

Fake jobs asking for money or personal information.

🎭

Impersonation

Someone pretends to be a trusted person.

📈

Investment Scam

Fake schemes promising unrealistic returns.

5 Simple Safety Rules

🔐 Use strong and unique passwords.

🔒 Turn on multi-factor authentication.

⏸️ Pause when a message creates urgency.

🔎 Verify links, callers and payment details.

🚫 Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.
